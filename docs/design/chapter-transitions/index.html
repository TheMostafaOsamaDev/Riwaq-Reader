Chapter transitions

Two questions on one page. §1 is settled work — how big a chapter's opening title gets as the reader drags the size slider, and where it stops. §2 is open — five directions for the block at the end of a chapter. Everything paints in the app's real tokens and the app's real faces (Markazi Text, Readex Pro), so a specimen here is a specimen of what ships.

§1Chapter opening title

The title has always been bodySize × 1.8 with no ceiling and no phone case. The slider runs 14–42, so at the top that is a 75.6px title on a phone column — three lines of display type before the chapter has started. The proposal ramps, plateaus, then tracks: it still answers the size choice, it stops before it wrecks the column, and it is never smaller than the text it introduces. The numbers are apparent sizes, converted back to font-size through the display face's measured correction — the title is set in Markazi, whose ink is only ~76% of the face the size slider is calibrated against, so a ceiling written in px means something else entirely by the time it reaches the glass.

Title size across the whole slider

Plotted in apparent size — the size the eye reads. The first version of this curve was written in font-size px and quietly shipped a phone title measuring 0.95× the body it opened; the dashed line is the body, and no curve may ever come within 10% of it — that floor is the hard rule, and the cap yields to it.

§2End of chapter — candidates

Every block below is E — a chapter turn with a pair of ways out under it. That shape is settled; the other directions are gone. What varies here is the geometry, and what carries the weight. The eyebrow, the title in the display face, the context line and the chevron are shared code, so the variants differ by idea rather than by drift, and the baseline is kept at the top only so the difference is visible.

Decided: E · Marginal pair — implemented in src/components/ChapterEnd.tsx. The rest stay here as the record of what it was chosen against.

The four that try something: Spine gives the chapter numeral a full-height band on the leading edge, so it reads as the edge of a volume rather than a badge. Leaves puts two offset sheets behind the card — the affordance is depth, which is the one metaphor a reader of a 2372-chapter book already has, and it costs no height. Ghost numeral bleeds the number off the trailing edge as texture at 7% ink, so it never competes with the title. Ticket stub replaces the internal border with a perforation, notches and all. Side rail turns the pair 90° into a trailing column, buying back ~46px of page at the exact moment a reader wants the next chapter and not furniture. Marginal pair is the quietest: only the turn is a control, and the two secondary moves are set as marginalia in the page's own voice.
