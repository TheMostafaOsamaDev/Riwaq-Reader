Riwaq reads the books you already own: EPUBs, scanned PDFs, .docx drafts, translated web novels, whatever language they happen to be written in.

Arabic gets the same care as English rather than a mirrored stylesheet and a shrug. Six of the sixteen bundled reading faces are Naskh or Kufi, diacritics render properly, and switching the interface to Arabic moves the sidebar, flips the icons and translates every label.

Nothing leaves your device. There is no account to make, nothing to sync, and no telemetry of any kind. Your library is plain files on your own storage.

READING

* EPUB 2 and 3, PDF, and .docx
* Two pages, one page, or a continuous scroll
* Tap to turn, with zones you can resize
* 16 bundled faces across Naskh, modern sans, Kufi and display, each corrected so the size slider means the same thing whichever you pick
* Size, line height, letter spacing, paragraph spacing, width, alignment and hyphenation
* Light, Sepia, Dark and a true black for OLED, or follow the system
* Focus mode, which takes the toolbars and the system bars away and tells you how to come back

FINDING YOUR PLACE

* Contents grouped by volume, with chapter search
* A marker for where you are, and a jump back to it
* A scrubber across the whole book
* Highlights in four colours, with notes, collected in one place with the chapter each came from

THE STORE

Riwaq can browse Arabic web novel sites from inside the app, read them online, or download chapters for later. Sources are installable extensions rather than a fixed list, so a site can be added, updated or removed without waiting for a new version of the app, and you can point Riwaq at your own repository. Installing an extension asks for confirmation first, because it runs with the app's reach.

A downloaded novel can be baked into a standalone EPUB, either as one file or one per volume, and it lands in your library beside everything else.

Riwaq hosts and redistributes nothing. The Store reads publicly available pages so you can read them offline. Please support the translators and the official releases where they exist.
