New

* The book page has one main button, Read. Download range and Shelves are icons beside it, and a ⋮ menu holds Save as offline book and Remove from library.
* On the phone, tap the page to hide the bars, tap again to bring them back. Scrolling hides them too.
* The focus mode notice is now a thin bar, shown once on entry.

Fixes

* Flatpak installs no longer offer an update; Flathub handles them.
* F-Droid can now offer the very APK we sign: the Android build is reproducible.
